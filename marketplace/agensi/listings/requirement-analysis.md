## Detailed description

Converts business requirements into structured engineering requirements - functional and non-functional requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, assumption and risk registers, clarification questions and a traceability matrix. Use when a feature, project or ticket is described in business terms and needs to be made testable before design or coding starts.

Turn an ambiguous business request into requirements an engineer can design against and a tester can verify. Requirements come before architecture and code.
