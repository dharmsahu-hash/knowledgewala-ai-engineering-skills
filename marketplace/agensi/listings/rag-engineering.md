## Detailed description

Designs and reviews retrieval-augmented generation systems - document ingestion, chunking, embedding and vector store selection, retrieval strategies, hybrid search, reranking, metadata filtering, evaluation and hallucination reduction. Use when building a question-answering or search assistant over private documents, or diagnosing poor retrieval or ungrounded answers.

Build RAG systems whose answers are grounded, cited and measurable, and fix retrieval problems with evidence rather than guesses.
