## Detailed description

Designs and optimises databases - relational modelling for MySQL, PostgreSQL and Aurora, indexing, query analysis with EXPLAIN, transactions and isolation, connection pooling, caching with Redis, DynamoDB access patterns, and safe, reversible migrations. Use when designing a schema, tuning slow queries, planning a data migration, or choosing a caching or datastore strategy.

Produce correct schemas, justified indexes and safe migrations, and tune performance from evidence (query plans and measurements).
