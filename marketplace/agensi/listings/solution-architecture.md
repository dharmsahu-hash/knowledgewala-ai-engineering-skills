## Detailed description

Designs scalable, maintainable solution architecture - pattern selection, monolith versus microservices assessment, service boundaries, integration and event-driven design, resilience, cost and operations - and records decisions as ADRs with Mermaid diagrams. Use when approved requirements exist and a technology or structural decision must be made before detailed design or coding.

Choose an architecture that fits the requirements, team and budget, and make every significant decision explicit, reasoned and reversible where possible.
